WHAT VOWVY IS

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Vowvy is a searchable memory system for physical possessions.

Not inventory software. Not a moving checklist. Not a storage management tool.
A memory system. You photograph what matters, and later you can find it.

The difference is emotional: Vowvy treats your belongings the way your memory
treats them — as things that have meaning, context, and history. Not SKUs.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CORE PHILOSOPHY

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

"Capture first. Organize later."

The enemy of memory is friction. If it takes effort to record something,
people don't do it — and then they lose it.

Vowvy removes the effort. You open the app, take a photo, and you're done.
AI handles the tagging. Organization is optional, not required.
The system rewards impulsive documentation, not careful cataloging.

This philosophy governs every product decision:
- Fewer taps to capture = more things captured
- Search beats folder hierarchies
- A photo with no tags is better than a perfect record that never got made

WHO VOWVY IS FOR

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PRIMARY USERS

  The Overwhelmed Mover
    Moving house, packing boxes, no idea what's in them.
    Needs: fast capture, QR codes on boxes, searchable later.
    Pain: unpacking mystery boxes six months after moving in.

  The Downsizer
    Clearing out a home — their own or a parent's. Decades of stuff.
    Needs: record what exists before deciding what to keep, sell, or donate.
    Pain: making irreversible decisions about things they can't see.

  The Declutterer
    Doing a deep clear-out. Wants to feel in control of the process.
    Needs: a record of what they had, what they kept, what they let go.
    Pain: regretting things they can't remember getting rid of.

  The Grieving Family
    Dealing with a loved one's estate. Emotional and overwhelming.
    Needs: documentation that doesn't require judgment calls in the moment.
    Pain: having to decide what everything is worth while still grieving.

SECONDARY USERS

  Families Preserving Memory
    Grandma's china. The old tools in the garage. The jewelry box.
    Objects with meaning that should be documented before they're dispersed.

  Collectors
    Books, records, art, wine, tools — anything collected and stored.
    Needs: searchable catalog with photos, no complex database setup.

  Small Businesses
    Studio inventory, prop rooms, equipment storage, supply closets.
    Needs: team access, shared visibility, quick lookup.

WHAT VOWVY IS NOT

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

- Not a home inventory app for insurance (though it works for that)
- Not a moving company software package
- Not a storage unit management system
- Not a warehouse tool
- Not for anything requiring barcodes, SKUs, or quantity tracking

If someone needs any of those things, there are better tools.
Vowvy is for the things that have meaning, not just value.

COMPETITIVE POSITION

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Home inventory apps (Encircle, Memento, Sortly):
  → Complex, designed for insurance claims, require manual categorization
  → Vowvy wins on speed of capture and emotional tone

Moving apps (MoveAdvisor, Updater):
  → Focused on logistics, not memory
  → Vowvy wins on what happens after the move

Note-taking apps (Notion, Apple Notes):
  → No photo-first flow, no AI tagging, no QR codes
  → Vowvy wins on specificity and zero-friction capture

Vowvy's moat: the combination of photo-first capture + AI tagging + QR codes
on physical objects is genuinely novel in this space. Nobody else is doing
all three for personal/household use.
