Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

What is Vowvy?

Vowvy is a personal inventory app that helps you photograph and search the contents of physical containers, boxes, and storage spaces.

What data we collect

  • Your email address, used to create and manage your account
  • Photos you upload of your containers and their contents
  • Location labels and container names you create (e.g. "Garage", "Blue Bin 1")
  • AI-generated tags and descriptions created from your photos

How your data is used

  • To provide the Vowvy service — storing your inventory and making it searchable
  • Photos are analyzed by Google's Gemini AI to generate searchable tags
  • We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for any purpose other than providing the service

Who can see your data

Only you. Your inventory is private by default. No one else can access your containers, photos, or tags unless you explicitly share them.

Third-party services

Vowvy is built on Google Firebase (authentication, database, file storage). Photos are processed by Google Gemini AI. This site uses Google reCAPTCHA v3 to protect against bots — reCAPTCHA use is subject to Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Data storage

Your data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure in the United States.

Deleting your data

You can delete any container, photo, or your entire account at any time. To request full account deletion, email us at privacy@vowvy.com.

Contact

Questions? Email privacy@vowvy.com.
